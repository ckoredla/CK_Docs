# Distribution pack: How AI Is Quietly Transforming Aircraft Maintenance

## LinkedIn post

AI in aircraft maintenance is often described as a prediction problem.

That is incomplete.

The real challenge is building a trustworthy decision system around the model:

- reliable aircraft and maintenance data
- explainable signals
- integration with existing workflows
- qualified human review
- feedback from actual maintenance outcomes

A model can identify a pattern. It cannot, by itself, create operational trust.

In my first article for Aviation AI Modernization, I explain a practical architecture for moving from fragmented telemetry and maintenance history to evidence-backed insights that technicians and engineering teams can use.

The central principle: start with the operational decision, not the AI model.
